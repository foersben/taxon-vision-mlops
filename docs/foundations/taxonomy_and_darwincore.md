# Taxonomy & DarwinCore Standards

The platform organizes biological observations according to hierarchical Linnaean taxonomy:

$$\text{Kingdom} \to \text{Phylum} \to \text{Class} \to \text{Order} \to \text{Family} \to \text{Genus} \to \text{Species}$$

Each observation conforms to the international **DarwinCore** biodiversity standard.
