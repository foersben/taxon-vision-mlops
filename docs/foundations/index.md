# Chapter 1: Foundations

Welcome to the foundational chapter of the TaxonVision-MLOps documentation. This section introduces the core domain concepts required to process and understand global biodiversity data.

Before delving into neural networks and inference optimization, it is critical to understand the constraints of the data we are modeling. This chapter exhaustively covers the biological taxonomy paradigms, the structure of DarwinCore observational data, and the real-world scale of the iNaturalist data ecosystem. Finally, it contrasts traditional academic machine learning curricula against the rigorous, decoupled production strategies necessary for mission-critical deployments.
